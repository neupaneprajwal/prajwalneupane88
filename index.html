ABOUT ME

✉ in 🎓 📷

PhD Candidate - Southern Methodist University, Dallas, USA

MS - Tribhuvan University, Nepal

BS - Tri-Chandra Multiple Campus, Tribhuvan University, Nepal

I am a Geoscientist specializing in earthquake seismology, exploration seismology, and active tectonics. My recent research focuses on earthquake source characterization and crustal stress analysis in the New Madrid Seismic Zone, subsurface fault mapping in Butte Valley, Northern California, and ambient noise analysis in the New Madrid Seismic Zone.

My expertise spans seismic data acquisition, processing, and interpretation, including multichannel seismic surveying, seismic station deployment, and monitoring. I also bring hands-on geological mapping experience from fieldwork in the Himalayan terrain.

My long-term goal is to apply my geophysical and geological expertise to solving complex subsurface exploration problems.
